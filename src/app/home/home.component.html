Attracting and managing human capital is among the top priorities of today's successful companies. Accomplishing this requires a business partner that understands your culture, your industry and knows how to identify and engage exceptional talent. We also recruit professionals to work directly for you from the start as full-time, permanent employees.

We have an exemplary track record of working with professionally managed Indian companies and MNCs. Our recruitment procedures and methods are designed to meet 2 principal objectives:

Contract / Contract to Hire

We also deploy candidates on project specific need i.e. for short-term or long term assignments.

We undertake the mobilization of personnel in the specified numbers and their placement at client-identified locations through our own team of committed Staffing Solutions members. On request, or at the conclusion of the contract period, the deputed personnel are demobilized from the client sites or can be available for the permanent position. Clients thus experience the performance and contribution of deputed personnel while enjoying the flexibility and freedom to focus on their core business. In addition, all AZ7 Global deputees are on our rolls, assuring them of all the benefits and the dignity of a regular employee in a respected company.

  • The opportunity for organizations to focus on core areas
  • Rapid & flexible deployment of resources
  • Ease of recruitment and replacement
  • Long-term cost advantages
  • Reduce HR Administration Function & Cost (Payroll, Accounting, Benefits, Training etc.)
  • Retain & attract talent
